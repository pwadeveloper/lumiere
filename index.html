    Good to know

    What keeps us both protected.

    How do the deposit and balance work?

    A minimum 80% deposit is required before your date is confirmed. The remaining 20% is due no later than the day of the event. Paying any deposit or invoice means you agree to Lumière by Bee’s full Terms & Conditions.

    When do I get my photos?

    The First Chapter and The Love Story: soft copies within 3 weeks. Happily Ever After: within 2 weeks. All photobooks and framed prints are produced within 30 working days after the balance is settled.

    What if we need to cancel or change the date?

    Deposits are non-refundable but transferable to a new date with at least 30 days’ notice. Changes within 30 days depend on availability. If the photographer can’t fulfil the booking, your deposit is refunded in full.

    Do you shoot outside your area?

    Yes. For weddings outside the primary area, you cover transport and accommodation, confirmed in writing before the shoot.

    Can we add more coverage hours?

    Yes, arranged in advance at an extra charge.

    What’s in the couples portrait session?

    The Love Story includes a 45-minute uninterrupted session. Happily Ever After includes 90 minutes, plus a complimentary pre-wedding shoot that doesn’t reduce the package price.

    Will you post our photos?

    Selected images may be shared on social platforms unless you say otherwise in the Client Details Form.
