    Good to know

    What keeps us both protected.

    How do the deposit and balance work?

    A minimum 80% deposit is required before your date is confirmed. The remaining 20% is due on the day of the session.

    When do I get my photos?

    Soft copies are delivered within 3 weeks of the session.

    When do prints arrive?

    All photobooks and framed prints are produced within 30 working days after the balance is settled.

    How do outfit changes work?

    Allow 10–15 minutes for each change. That time is factored into the session, so there is no rush between looks.

    Where does the session happen?

    Studio sessions take place in the photographer's studio. Outdoor sessions happen at a location agreed in advance. Travel outside the primary area is charged separately.

    What if we need to cancel or change the date?

    Deposits are non-refundable but transferable to a new date with at least 14 days' notice.

    Will you post our photos?

    Selected images may be shared on social platforms unless you say otherwise before the session.

    What am I agreeing to?

    Paying any deposit or invoice means you agree to Lumière by Bee's full Terms & Conditions.
